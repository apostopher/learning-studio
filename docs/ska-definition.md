# SKA Profile Definition

## Knowledge

### Definition

Facts, concepts, and theories learned through school, books, or work.

### Examples

- Software rules
- Safety laws
- Math facts

## Skills

### Definition

Actions and tasks you can actively do, built through practice.

### Examples

- Typing fast
- Writing code
- Speaking a foreign language
- Flying a plane

## Attitudes

### Definition

Traits of character that influence how you think, feel, and behave in different situations.

### Examples

- Being a team player
- Being a good listener
- Being a good communicator
- Being a good problem solver
- Being a good decision maker
- Being a good leader
- Being a good follower
- Being a good team leader
- Being a good team follower
- Being a good team leader
