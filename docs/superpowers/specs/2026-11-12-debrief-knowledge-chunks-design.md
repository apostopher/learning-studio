# Debrief by knowledge chunks — design

Date: 2026-11-12. Status: draft for approval.

## Why

A debrief exists to teach (the essay-sets redesign of 2026-11-11 made that the stated purpose). Today it measures one attempt as a whole: three essays, plus three more under par, an average against 75. A pilot who gets 60% learns their number. They don't learn which ideas they haven't grasped, and nothing makes their next attempt work on those ideas.

The new model: a lesson teaches a fixed set of **knowledge chunks**. A chunk is one atomic key point taught in the video. The debrief tracks each pilot's grip on each chunk. Every later attempt goes after the chunks they haven't cleared yet (**knowledge holes**), until every chunk is cleared. Then the debrief is done at 100%.

## Goals

- Every lesson with a debrief has a stable, stored list of knowledge chunks, generated by AI.
- Every debrief question is tagged with the chunk(s) it tests. Internal only: pilots never see tags.
- Answers are graded against the chunks they're tagged with, giving each pilot a score per chunk.
- A chunk is **cleared** once the pilot scores **≥ 70** on it. Cleared is permanent.
- The first attempt covers every chunk. Each later attempt focuses on the pilot's holes plus a little review.
- The debrief is passed (shown as 100%) the moment every chunk is cleared, even mid-attempt.
- Between attempts the pilot sees mastery (chunks cleared ÷ total), a readable "Worth revisiting" list and a live countdown to the next attempt.
- Answering behaviour is logged for later study.
- Pasting into the answer box is blocked.

## Non-goals (v1)

- Using the answering-behaviour logs to detect difficulty. They are logged only.
- Admin review or editing of chunks. Chunks are AI-generated and not curated.
- Attempt limits. Attempts continue every 6 hours until the debrief is passed.
- Changing the first debrief clock: it still opens 12h after the video is fully watched (or the lesson is done, for no-video lessons), per `debriefClockStart`.

## Anti-goals

- **A pilot must never lose a cleared chunk** through anything the pilot did, including a weak answer on a review question. The only exception is regeneration, below.
- **Mastery must never be shown as anything but "chunks cleared ÷ total"**, wherever a debrief score appears. Two different numbers for the same debrief would be a defect.
- **Notes must not be raw dumps** of chunk names or grader judgements. "Worth revisiting" is readable prose aimed at the pilot.
- **No sub-AA contrast and no unlabelled locked states.** Every locked or waiting state says why and when it opens (project rules).

## Decisions made with the user

1. **Chunk source.** AI generates the chunks from the lesson's **key points plus the video transcript**. Key points are the anchor; the transcript adds context.
2. **Cleared is permanent.** Once a chunk reaches ≥ 70 it stays cleared. Later weak answers on it don't reopen it.
3. **First attempt covers every chunk.**
4. **Later attempts** focus on holes and include a few already-cleared chunks.
5. **Passing:** every chunk at ≥ 70 → the debrief is passed and shown as 100%, ending the debrief at once.
6. **Retake timing:** every attempt after the first opens **6 hours** after the previous one. The rewatch-to-redo rule (`rewatch-required`, `videoRewatchedAtSql`, `REWATCH_COPY`) is removed. Video and no-video lessons follow the same rule.
7. **After an attempt with holes, the pilot sees:**
   - a "Worth revisiting" list in readable text, not a list of names;
   - a message that the next attempt opens in 6 hours, as a live countdown ("5 h 40 min" and so on).
8. **Score shown = mastery so far:** 5 of 7 chunks cleared = 71%.
9. **Answering behaviour is logged, not used in v1:** time taken, erase-and-rewrite activity, and similar signals.
10. **No copy-paste into the answer input.**
11. **Regenerated chunks:** when a lesson's chunks change, a pilot keeps the cleared chunks that still match a new chunk. Everything else resets to not cleared. This applies only to pilots still working through the debrief (see 13).
12. **A half-finished attempt is resumable.**
    - Every graded answer is saved as it is given, and chunks it clears stay cleared.
    - The answer being typed is auto-saved with a debounce, so a closed tab or dropped connection loses nothing.
    - The pilot can come back at any time and continue the same attempt: same questions, with their draft restored.
    - The 6-hour wait starts only when the attempt is finished, meaning every question is answered or the short circuit has ended it.
    - An open attempt doesn't expire. A pilot can't get a fresh set of questions by walking away.
13. **A pass is permanent.**
    - Once a pilot has passed, whether under this system or the old one, no later change to the lesson takes it back. They stay at 100%, with no returning "D" badge and no drop in course percent.
    - Regeneration (decision 11) only adjusts mastery for pilots who haven't passed yet.

## Calls I made (the user hasn't confirmed these)

- **Chunk count:** at most 8 per lesson. This matches the existing `KEY_POINTS_MAX`. If a lesson has more, the generator merges its least important chunks rather than dropping coverage.
- **First attempt size:** one question per chunk, so 7 chunks means 7 questions. A question may also be tagged with a second chunk. The two-set structure (`debrief-sets.ts`, `DEBRIEF_SET_SIZE`, second set below par) is retired.
- **Later attempt size:** one fresh question per hole, written from a new angle and never a reword of a question the pilot has already seen, plus 1–2 cleared chunks as review. If there are more holes than 8, cap at 8 by keeping the lowest-scoring ones.
- **A chunk's score** is its score from the latest question that tested it, unless it is already cleared. When a question is tagged with two chunks, the grader judges each chunk separately for that answer.
- **Review questions** are graded and logged, and their misses can appear in "Worth revisiting". They can never reopen a cleared chunk.
- **Mid-attempt short circuit:** questions come one at a time. As soon as the last hole is cleared, the attempt ends and the passed report is shown. The remaining questions are skipped.
- **Matching after regeneration:** an AI step maps old chunks to new ones by meaning, not by wording. A cleared old chunk carries its cleared status to the new chunk it matches. Mastery is then recalculated against the new total (5 of 7 may become 4 of 8).
- **Regeneration trigger:** when the debrief source fingerprint changes (key points, material text or transcript). This uses the same mechanism `prepared-debrief.server.ts` uses today.
- **Existing pilots:**
  - Anyone who already passed a debrief under the old system stays passed, permanently (decision 13).
  - Anyone with only below-par attempts starts from zero mastery: old attempts carry no chunk tags. Their next attempt is a full first attempt, opening 6 hours after their last one, or immediately if that is already past.
  - The `DEBRIEF_FORMAT` bump retires every debrief prepared in the old format.
- **The 75 pass mark goes away** for debriefs. The pass is per chunk at 70. `DEBRIEF_PAR_SCORE` is replaced, not kept alongside.
- **Same number everywhere:** the score report, the lesson ring's debrief badge, the lesson-list "D" state and the course percent all read mastery. In particular, the sidebar computes debrief state in the browser (`computeDebriefState` in `debrief-schedule.ts`), so it needs the same facts the server uses.
- **"Worth revisiting" content:** one short paragraph per hole, written by the grader from the pilot's own answer: what the idea is, and what their answer missed or got wrong. Cleared chunks are not listed. Viper (`findRecentDebriefGaps`) is given the same notes instead of today's raw idea list.
- **Countdown:**
  - It ticks while the page is open, every minute, and every second under a minute. It shows the exact clock time underneath.
  - When it reaches zero, the tab refetches and opens, the same way `availableAt` refetches today.
  - Screen readers hear it once on load and once when it opens, not on every tick.
- **Paste blocking:**
  - The answer box rejects paste and drop of text. A short inline note says why ("Answers have to be typed — pasting is turned off for debriefs").
  - Each blocked attempt is logged.
  - Copying and cutting out of the answer box are blocked too.
  - Copying the question text stays allowed. The user didn't ask to block it, and blocking it gets in the way of selecting text and of assistive tools.
  - Dictation and on-screen keyboards are unaffected, because they insert typed input, not paste.
- **Logged per answer:**
  - time from question shown to submit;
  - time to first keystroke;
  - number of deletions and characters deleted;
  - number of times the answer was cleared or mostly rewritten (more than half deleted);
  - paste attempts blocked;
  - time the tab was hidden while answering;
  - final answer length.

  This is stored with the attempt and has no reader in v1. Per the project's testing rule, it should get a comment naming its future consumer.

## What exists today (for the builder)

- **Generation:** `src/ai/generate-test.ts`. Generate with Sonnet, check quality with Haiku, regenerate failures. The prompt is `src/ai/prompts/generation.ts`. Questions already carry `keyPointIndex` (`src/ai/schemas.ts`).
- **Source:** `src/lib/lesson-debrief-source.server.ts`.
  - **Today authored material wins and the transcript is only a fallback**, with key points derived by `src/ai/derive-key-points.ts`. Decision 1 changes this: key points plus transcript.
  - **My call, the chunk source rule:**
    - The anchor is the authored key points. If a lesson has none, key points are derived from the material text, and failing that from the transcript, as today.
    - The transcript is added as context whenever one exists. The material text is used as context when there is no transcript.
    - A lesson can have a different transcript in each course, because `getLessonTranscript` resolves through each course's credentials. The chunks are built once, from the transcript of the lesson's home course, so every course shares one chunk list.
    - Questions are written from the chunks plus that same context.
- **Preparing ahead:** `src/lib/prepared-debrief.server.ts`.
  - Per pilot, stored in Postgres, fingerprinted by `DEBRIEF_FORMAT` and the source text.
  - It is prepared during video playback and again after a below-par attempt. That second run is where a holes-focused attempt gets generated.
- **Grading:** `src/ai/evaluate-answer.ts` and `src/ai/prompts/evaluation.ts`.
  - The grader invents 1–5 `essentialIdeas` per answer and marks each conveyed fully, partly or not. `scoreFreeTextEval` turns that into 0–100 (80% of ideas = 100, with an incorrect-claim penalty).
  - Per-chunk grading should judge against the tagged chunks rather than invented ideas, but the same judgement-then-score approach is a good fit.
- **Attempts:** `lesson_test_results` stores questions, answers, `total_score` and `completed_at`.
  - There is no per-chunk mastery store yet. One is needed, per user × lesson × chunk, and it must survive regeneration (decision 11).
  - **Prod schema changes are migrated by hand, never with `db:push`** (6,917 embedding rows are at risk from unrelated drift). The order must be migrate → deploy → backfill.
- **Timing and state:**
  - `src/lib/debrief-schedule.ts` has `computeDebriefState`, `DEBRIEF_DELAY_MS` and the `retake-waiting` / `rewatch-required` states. The rollup SQL is in `src/db/progress-components.ts` and the route facts are in `src/db/lesson-test.ts`.
  - A 6-hour retake delay is new and is separate from the 12-hour first-open delay.
- **UI:**
  - `src/components/lesson-material/parts/debrief-wait.tsx` shows the waiting states.
  - `question-card.tsx` holds the answer input.
  - `debrief-panel-container.tsx` and `computeDebriefPanel` drive the tab.
  - `score-report` shows "Worth revisiting" (`src/lib/debrief-gaps.ts`).

## Left to the builder

- The schema for chunks and per-pilot chunk mastery, and where the behaviour logs live.
- Prompt wording for chunk generation, holes-focused question generation, per-chunk grading and the "Worth revisiting" notes.
- Whether chunk generation is its own step with its own cache, or part of debrief preparation, as long as every pilot on a lesson shares the same chunk list.
- The exact edit-tracking heuristics, within the list above.
- Copy wording, held to the existing tone ("We will debrief this KC tomorrow.").
