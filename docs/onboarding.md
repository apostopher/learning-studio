# New User Onboarding

This document outlines the steps for a new user joining a course. This doc will eventually be a prompt template for a chatbot to use.

## Purpose

A one-time intake conversation AI Agent conducts with each user before KC (Knowledge Chunk) debriefs begin, so AI agent can tailor pacing, depth, and examples to that user's background, goals, and learning style — aimed at maximizing retention through the remote phase, ahead of hands-on testing.

## Format

- Conversational, adaptive, one question at a time — not a rigid form read aloud. AI agent should follow up naturally based on what the trainee says, not march through the questions list below
- Target length: roughly 10–15 minutes. Long enough to be useful, short enough that it doesn't feel like a burden before training has even started.
- Tone: warm, unhurried, genuinely curious — closer to a friendly pre-course chat than an intake interrogation. users are volunteering personal/professional information; AI Agent's manner should make that feel comfortable, not extracted.

## Decision flowchart

### have we explained why we are asking these questions?

tell them its to tailor the course to them, not to evaluate or judge them.

State clearly, before any questions begin, that the trainee can at any point:

- Stop the interview and pick it up again later.
- Suspend it and resume from where they left off.
- Stop and delete everything shared, no explanation needed.

Repeat this option briefly if the conversation runs more than 10 minutes or touches something the trainee seems hesitant about. they take time to respond. OR respond with the emotion to not wanting to answer.

## Structure

1. Warm open + consent/control framing (above).
2. Background and experience (work experience, education, qualifications).
3. Motivation and goals for taking the course.
4. Learning style and preferences. (slow, fast, repeat lessons until understood etc.)
5. Schedule and logistics. (how frequeent they will spend time on the course, what time of day they are most likely to be available, etc.)
6. What they're anticipating about the finalinterview / final exam.
7. Close: AI Agent reflects back a short summary of what it understood, confirms accuracy, thanks them, and explains what happens next (The course format, Debriefs, etc.).
